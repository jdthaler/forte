The bar C4

The struck bar to scale (thickness ×4) on its two blocks, over its box, and its first three bending modes moving with their live amplitudes, slowed down. ▼ hammer, ▬ damper felt; the box fills with colour as it takes the bar’s energy.

Bar and box

barbox
The energy in the bar and in the air of its box over the last 3 s (60 dB tall). Close together they trade it back and forth, and the box radiates it: that is what makes a celesta note short and sweet.
