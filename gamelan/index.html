The gamelan

The struck key’s octave of the instrument: pélog’s seven bars over sléndro’s five (or twelve in a row), over their tubes on a gangsa, or the kettles and gongs with their bosses. Each glows as it rings; a pair’s two partners swell and fade together at the beat.

Mode shapes and the strike

A bar’s first bending modes along its length and its first twisting mode, and where the mallet lands (dashed): on a node a mode stays silent. A gong’s modes from above, their nodal circles and diameters, the boss at the centre and the beater’s spot.
