The planet, ringing

The ground rising (gold) and falling (blue) after the last quake, from its lowest modes, slowed so that ₀S₂ swings in 1.6 s and hugely exaggerated: the football mode squashes the planet one way, then the other, while the spin turns its pattern. ★ the quake, ▲ the station.

Inside

The model the modes were solved for, coloured by the speed of shear waves (bluer: faster); a liquid core carries no shear at all, so the twisting (toroidal) modes stop at it.
