Pickup capacitance C(y)

Reed–comb capacitance vs the reed's height above the comb's mid-plane, from Laplace's equation. Band: this strike's swing; line: the rest height. Below: the comb voltage it produces. A swing that reaches over the top of the curve folds the waveform: even harmonics, the bark.
