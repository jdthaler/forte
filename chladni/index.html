Plate A4 and its sand

The key's plate from above, clamped at its centre, with sand on it: wherever the plate shakes harder than gravity the grains hop about, and they come to rest on the nodal lines, which stay still. The bow is drawn down across the edge at the dark bar; the two circles are fingertips resting on the figure's nodal lines. Each key is its own plate, with its own sand; tap the plate twice to sprinkle it afresh.

Chladni's figures

The nodal lines of the ten figures on this plate, lowest first, with each one's pitch over the plate's lowest mode. The figure the keys are cut for is framed; the one sounding now is filled.
