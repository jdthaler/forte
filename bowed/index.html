The string on the wheel, C4

The string's shape, rebuilt from its travelling waves and sampled stroboscopically (each frame 1/48 of a period later). In Helmholtz motion it is two straight lines meeting at a corner that runs round the dashed parabolas once per period: it sticks to the wheel while the corner is on the far side and slips while the corner passes between wheel and bridge.

Schelleng diagram

Helmholtz motion needs Fmin < F < Fmax, with Fmax ∝ vB/β and Fmin ∝ vB/β². The dot is this key now: its force (press harder, use aftertouch or ↑↓) and the wheel position. Its colour is what the string is actually doing.

Velocity of the string under the wheel

stick and slip
Stuck, the string's surface moves with the wheel (vB, upper line); once per period it slips back at −vB(1 − β)/β (lower line) for a fraction β of the period. Extra dips are extra slips: surface sound below Fmin, chaos above Fmax.
