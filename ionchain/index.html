The crystal of C4

The ions as a camera on the trap sees them, glowing in the cooling light, each smeared over its own motion (exaggerated): a kicked ion shakes its neighbours, since the chain rings only as a whole. The gold bars are the electrodes, when they are close enough to show; the violet line is the laser pulse. Weaken the radial pull (the mod wheel) and the line buckles into a zigzag.
