Frequency against wind speed for one string. The curve is its Strouhal frequency St·U/D; the horizontal lines are its harmonics. Where the curve crosses a harmonic, the dark band is where the linear analysis finds the string and its wake unstable together (lock-in); the pale extension is how far a string already locked holds on (hysteresis). The dot is the wind now, with its gusts trailing.
