# the FC-300 behind a MIDI interface

### Forte (minor)
- **Say which device the FC-300 is on:** a Roland FC-300 comes through a MIDI interface (a UM-ONE), whose name says nothing of it, and a page only knew it once CTL 1, CTL 2 or a toe switch was pressed: until then it had no panel in the MIDI devices drawer and its EXP 2 was a mod wheel, not Morph. Each device in the drawer's *Plugged in* list now has a small menu, *MIDI device* or *Roland FC-300*: choose the FC-300 on its interface and it has its panel and EXP 2 moves Morph at once. The browser remembers it, and choosing *MIDI device* takes it away again (its switches then no longer bring it back on that interface).
- **Found by itself where SysEx is allowed:** where the browser already lets the page talk SysEx (after *Let Forte drive it*, or a Seaboard RISE's settings), the page asks every port for an FC-300 as MIDI starts and finds it without a pedal pressed, in whichever mode it was left.
