# transpose and octave

### Forte (minor)
- **Octave and Transpose for MIDI keyboards, on every page and in every plugin:** two menus beside the MIDI status (after MPE's) move the MIDI keys up to four octaves and twelve semitones either way, so a 25-key controller reaches all 88 keys, or a piece fingered in one key sounds in another. Every page shares them, as it does the velocity curve and MPE, and they show in the instrument's colour while they move the keys; turning MIDI on says so when they do. A note held while they change ends where it began, as do its poly pressure and its MPE bend and slide; a note moved past MIDI's 0–127 plays nothing. The keys on the screen and the computer keys play what they show. The plugins have them as Transpose and Octave parameters (in the MIDI group of the window, saved with the project, after MPE), moving CLAP's note events and note expressions too, and the Forte app keeps them when the instrument changes.
