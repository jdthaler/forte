# transpose and octave

### Forte (minor)
- **Octave and Transpose for MIDI keyboards, on every page and in every plugin:** they move the MIDI keys up to four octaves and twelve semitones either way, so a 25-key controller reaches all 88 keys, or a piece fingered in one key sounds in another. A note held while they change ends where it began, as do its poly pressure and its MPE bend and slide; a note moved past MIDI's 0–127 plays nothing. The keys on the screen and the computer keys play what they show. The plugins have them as Transpose and Octave parameters (in the window's MIDI group, saved with the project, after MPE), moving CLAP's note events and note expressions too, and the Forte app keeps them when the instrument changes.
- **The MIDI menu:** once MIDI is on, the MIDI status opens one menu with Touch (the velocity curve), MPE, Octave and Transpose, in place of the Touch and MPE pills beside it, so the header keeps to one line in a 900-pixel window and a phone can reach them too. Every page shares the four; while the keys are moved the status says how far, in the instrument's colour, and turning MIDI on says so in a toast.
