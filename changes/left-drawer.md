# a drawer on every instrument page, and keys that fold away

### Forte (minor)
- **A drawer at the left of every instrument page**, whether or not a Seaboard RISE is plugged in: the presets as a list, a tap each, the one playing lit (with your own presets and saving and sharing under them), then Morph, the RISE's panel while one is plugged in, and the groups every instrument has, moved out of the Controls panel: Output (the room, the venue and the volume), Tuning and Expression, and the MIDI settings (the MIDI status opens them). Each section folds away, and the page remembers which. On a desktop it is a column at the left that folds to a thin rail; in a narrower window or on an iPad held upright it opens over the views from that rail; on a phone it slides in from a button at the left of the header. Over the page it stops above the keys, so presets can be tried while playing. In Perform it starts folded, and the presets menu still sits in the keyboard bar.
- **The keys fold away**: a button at the left of the keyboard bar hides the on-screen keys (and their size and range buttons), leaving the bar with its volume and pedal, for playing from a MIDI keyboard; the views and the controls take the room. Every page remembers it.
- **The plugins' and the Forte app's window has the same drawer**: at its left, the presets as a list (the Presets menu in its header gives way to it while the drawer is open), then Morph, Output, Tuning, Expression, MIDI and CPU, each folding; its chevron folds it to a rail. The window's keys fold away from the button at the left of their bar too.
